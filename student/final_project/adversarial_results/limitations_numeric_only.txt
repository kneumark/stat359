Limitations and Design Decisions
================================

Scope
-----
This study initially explored additional prompt dimensions (authority ladder and tone ladder) on top of numeric perturbations.

Observed Limitation
-------------------
The authority/tone prompt variants produced degenerate generations (e.g., `Final Result : ERROR` or empty final result), leading to zero numeric parseability in verification.

Verification Evidence
---------------------
- Verification run summary: student/final_project/adversarial_results/verification_small/adversarial_numeric_summary_20260307_151442.json
- Result: parse success was 0.00% for all tested authority/tone combinations.

Compact Table
-------------
| Experiment variant                    | Parse success | Decision |
|--------------------------------------|---------------|----------|
| Numeric + authority/tone variants    | 0.00%         | Rejected |
| Numeric-only minimal Step 0 template | Restored (>0) | Accepted |

Final Causal Intervention Used
------------------------------
Model-facing adversarial prompt is restricted to:

Evaluate: <expression> <think>
Step 0: Claimed Result = <n>

No additional authority text, tone text, tags, anchor lines, or re-evaluate instructions are included in the final causal pipeline.
